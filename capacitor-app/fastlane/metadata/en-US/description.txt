YÖRÜNGE

Your comet circles a planet. Tap at the right moment, launch along the tangent and slip into the next planet's ring. Miss it and you drift into deep space.

Played with one finger, hard to put down.

▸ HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the screen: your comet launches off its orbit
• Fly into the next planet's ring to lock into orbit
• Jump before half a turn and your combo grows, multiplying your score
• Don't linger: if the ring turns red, the orbit collapses
• On your first run, on-screen markers show you how to aim

▸ FEATURES
• Six sectors followed by endless Deep Space, each with its own colors and music
• Checkpoints: restart from the sector you reached
• Hazards: orbiting moons, black holes and drifting planets
• Special planets: gold (gives stars), ice (spins slowly), pulsar (spins fast, bonus points)
• Power-ups: shield, magnet and 2x score
• World leaderboard: pick a nickname and put your best on the global list
• Three new missions every day and a 7-day reward streak
• Comet skins unlocked with the stars you collect
• Gentle reminders to come back (can be turned off in Settings)
• English and Turkish

No account needed. The game is free; it shows ads between runs and in exchange for rewards.

One more tap, one more orbit.
